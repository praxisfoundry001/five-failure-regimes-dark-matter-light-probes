# Five Failure Regimes in Dark-Matter Light Probes

## An Open-Source Pre-Result Demonstration Protocol

This repository contains a proposed experimental protocol for testing whether a five-way Structural Flow first-break classification can make useful, falsifiable predictions in dark-matter-sensitive light-probe settings.

**The experiment reported here has not been executed.**

The present artifact specifies the experimental architecture, information barriers, falsification structure, branch predictions, comparator burden, result taxonomy, and minimum requirements for a future independently executable implementation.

## What is being tested?

For an independently warranted dark-matter physical route, the proposal asks whether its first failed structural burden can be classified as:

**Dissolution · Shear · Drift · Echo · Lock**

and whether physically instantiated branches can yield pre-registered source and held-out light-probe predictions.

The number five refers to irreducible first-break classes under the declared foundational architecture.

It is **not** a prediction that astronomical observations contain exactly five image morphologies or five observable clusters.

## Experimental chain

The proposed experiment follows this information order:

**independently warranted dark-matter physical state
→ registered route and primary scale
→ intrinsic route-success test
→ first-break classification
→ frozen source prediction
→ ordinary gravitational forward model
→ held-out light prediction
→ measurement and reconstruction
→ comparison with a pre-registered ordinary-physics baseline
→ result adjudication**

Structural Flow contributes the classification and pre-result organization of the test.

It does not supply dark-matter dynamics, gravitational field equations, or a new propagation law for light.

## Current empirical status

This artifact is intentionally pre-result.

It does not report:

* a completed simulation campaign;
* numerical calibration;
* target unblinding;
* real-data analysis;
* empirical confirmation of any branch;
* an earned U₁ result;
* or an observed FR₆ result.

Those belong to future scientific executions.

## Current branch posture

Dissolution, Shear, and Drift have conditional dark-matter-facing source-to-light predictions proposed for an initial implementation.

Echo remains structurally valid but presently has no earned positive dark-matter physicalization in this program.

Lock remains conditional and is oriented toward simulation and synthetic-light testing rather than a clean real-observation claim.

PASS is the non-failure control and is not a sixth regime.

Those implementation materials are **specified by this proposal but are not included in the present publication**.

A future executor may instantiate them using appropriate scientific simulation, observational, and analysis tools while preserving the information barriers and adjudication rules stated in the manuscript.

## What can defeat the proposal?

A future branch may:

* fail to instantiate its proposed physical class;
* fail at the source-prediction layer;
* fail under ordinary gravitational propagation;
* fail at measurement or reconstruction;
* remain mixed or unresolved;
* fail to outperform the strong ordinary-physics comparator;
* or expose a genuine route-level residual that places pressure on the five-regime architecture.

Novel morphology alone does not establish a new Failure Regime.
